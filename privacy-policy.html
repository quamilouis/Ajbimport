01

Introduction

AJB Imports ("AJB Imports", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect when you visit our website, request a quotation, communicate with us, or use our logistics services.

Our commitment

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for providing, improving and managing our services.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with AJB Imports, we may collect different categories of information.

Personal Information

Name, email address, telephone number and other contact details.

Shipment Information

Origin, destination, cargo description, dimensions and related shipment details.

Technical Information

Browser, device, IP address and website interaction information.

03

Information You Provide

You may provide information directly to us when you:

  • Submit a quote request.
  • Contact our customer service team.
  • Request information about our services.
  • Provide shipment or cargo details.
  • Communicate with us by email, telephone, WhatsApp or other supported channels.
04

Shipment & Logistics Information

To provide logistics services, we may need information relating to a shipment.

This may include cargo descriptions, quantities, dimensions, weights, pickup locations, delivery locations, recipient information, shipping dates and other operational information.

Important

Customers are responsible for ensuring that information supplied to AJB Imports is accurate and complete.

05

Website & Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be automatically collected by our hosting, security, analytics or website technologies.

This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location, pages visited, referring website and general interaction information.

06

How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

01

Responding to enquiries and quotation requests.

02

Planning and delivering logistics services.

03

Communicating with customers about requested services.

04

Improving our website and customer experience.

05

Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.

06

Meeting applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

07

How We Share Information

We do not intend to sell personal information simply because you interact with our website.

Where necessary for providing services, information may be shared with trusted service providers, logistics partners, technology providers, professional advisers, government authorities or other parties where legally permitted or required.

We seek to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

08

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to help the website function, understand website usage and improve the user experience.

Depending on the technologies used on the website, you may be able to control certain cookies through your browser settings.

09

Data Security

AJB Imports takes reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure.

Security matters

No method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

10

Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements and meeting legal or regulatory obligations.

11

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Ask questions about how information is used.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Withdraw certain communications or marketing preferences.

Requests may be subject to applicable legal, contractual and operational limitations.

12

Marketing Communications

If you provide your contact details and agree to receive marketing communications, AJB Imports may contact you about relevant services, company updates or promotional information.

You may request that we stop sending non-essential marketing communications.

13

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses, organizations and individuals capable of entering into applicable agreements.

We do not knowingly design our logistics services or website to collect personal information from children.

14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, business operations or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact CoastBridge Logistics Ghana.
